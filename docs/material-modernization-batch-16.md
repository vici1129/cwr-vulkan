A future useful cap/reflector adoption would require new visible-benefit evidence.

and strong photographed edge shading. Geometry places it on crossed supports/

treatment and neutral lighting. Neither its name nor a timber-like shape resolves
every pixel. Original support appearance is retained without a guessed physical
class or generated material map.
